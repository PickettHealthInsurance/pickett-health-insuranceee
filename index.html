Helpful information

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer every plan available in my area?

No. Please see the notice at the top of this page for the number of organizations and products currently represented, and where to find information on all of your options.

Are you part of Medicare or the government?

No. Pickett Health Insurance is an independent insurance agency and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. government or Medicare.

What can we discuss in a consultation?

ACA/Marketplace coverage, Medicare-related coverage categories, life insurance, dental and vision coverage, or assistance for existing clients, based on options available in your area.

How can I get information on all of my Medicare options?

Visit Medicare.gov or call 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227).

Should I send my Medicare number or Social Security number through this website?

No. Do not send a Medicare number, Social Security number, or medical information through this website.
